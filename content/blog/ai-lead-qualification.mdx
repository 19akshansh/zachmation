Every inbound form submission is a lead someone has to manually read, judge,
and route. This template does that in seconds using Gemini, and posts the
result straight to your team's Discord channel.

## What this workflow does

1. **Google Form Trigger** fires when someone submits your form.
2. **Gemini** reads the response and scores the lead from 1–10 with a short
   reason.
3. **Discord Send** notifies your team with the score.

## Import it

Use the template card above - copy the JSON, then in Zachmation go to
**Workflows → Import** and paste it in. You'll need a Google Form credential
and a Gemini credential connected before running it.

## Customizing it

Swap the Discord node for Slack, Telegram, or an Email/SMTP node if that's
where your team lives. You can also add a **Filter** node after Gemini to
only notify on leads scoring above a threshold.
